# Persist exact indexed coverage outcomes and their provenance binding

- `CoverageCalibrationEvidenceRecord` schema v3 persists the complete exact-permutation coverage outcome ledger in declared replication order and a domain-separated SHA-256 binding of that ledger. Aggregate-identical runs that fail different prospectively declared DGP identities remain distinguishable and independently auditable; the digest is an additional compact provenance binding, not a replacement for the recoverable ledger.
- `validation_core::canonical_indexed_coverage_outcomes` continues to enforce the #730 identity contract: inputs must be an exact permutation of `0..attempted_replication_count`, while shard/completion order is normalized away. Malformed scientific coverage still fails through the existing aggregation owner before evidence construction.
- `validation_core::canonical_indexed_coverage_outcomes_sha256` hashes the declared attempt count and, in canonical replication order, each zero-based identity, success/failure state, successful window count, and exact IEEE-754 binary64 coverage bits under the `tepp.validation.coverage-calibration-outcomes.v1` domain. A pinned wire fixture, reversed completion order, changed failure identity, signed-zero bit drift, and duplicate identities cover the provenance contract.
- The digest owner uses the workspace-pinned `sha2` implementation; the evidence JSON exposes `replication_outcomes_sha256` alongside the full outcome ledger and exact source head.
- This change does not execute the prospective 10,000-DGP `tepp.coverage.nominal95.v1` × `tepp.simulation.rolling_origin_coverage.v1` study, define an acceptable numerical-failure rate, promote a scientific claim, or create a release.

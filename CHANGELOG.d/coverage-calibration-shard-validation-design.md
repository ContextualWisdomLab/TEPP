## Fixed

- Coverage-calibration shard schema v2 now binds each executed shard to the owner-issued prospective validation design identity and canonical SHA-256 in addition to simulation scenario, source head, ordinal range, and indexed outcomes. The design binding is derived from `validation_core` before scientific execution, included in the shard digest, preserved through strict JSON rehydration, and rechecked against the current owner-issued design during final assembly. Historical shards therefore cannot be reassessed under a post-outcome criterion change while still being represented as prospectively fixed evidence. Coverage, denominator, MCSE, percentile, and claim-assessment arithmetic remain owned by `validation_core`; this change does not execute the 10,000-DGP acceptance study or define numerical-failure acceptability.

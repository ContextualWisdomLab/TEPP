`validation_core` now derives the scientific-recovery exact-head receipt identity from a versioned binding of the tested Git head, immutable CI/test artifact SHA-256, and terminal receipt state. The underlying artifact digest remains separately available for audit, while `receipt_sha256()` and promoted scientific authority retain the derived binding identity so different heads or terminal states cannot share one receipt identity. This is a provenance binding only; it does not claim signature, signer, timestamp, or CI-attestation verification.

## Scientific recovery: simulator-backed rolling origins

- Added a simulator-backed repeated selected-`K` recovery contract that consumes digest-bound known-topic term counts, simulated weighted multiple Membership, observed transition edges, canonical leakage-safe expanding rolling-origin partitions, complete declared candidate-`K` fitting, fixed-training held-out predictive scoring, complete-horizon binding, and typed failure-denominator admission through the existing owners.
- The contract derives each cutoff only after all documents for one latent event are available, retains five strictly ordered out-of-temporal-sample windows per replication, and proves future evidence is refused by the cutoff-bound snapshot owner.
- The generated fixture also substitutes a later-window document identity into an earlier admitted evaluation and requires `PartitionInputMismatch`, so the scientific path cannot silently rebind future rows to a dimension-compatible held-out matrix.
- The CI-scale contract preserves relation-connected same-episode/derivative groups and checks that the generated corpus retains multiple-membership, cross-classified, and shared higher-level group structure before fitting.
- Four independent data-generating seeds are summarized through `selected_k_recovery_summary_from_results(...)`; every attempted replication remains in the denominator and successful replications must be sufficient to expose bias, RMSE, and their Monte Carlo standard errors.
- Truth-`K` topic-term recovery is globally aligned to the simulator topic basis for every admitted rolling-origin fit. RMSE remains cell-wise, while bias is computed per aligned topic-term parameter across windows and summarized by mean absolute parameter bias, preventing the simplex sum-to-one constraint from cancelling positive and negative topic-content errors into a false zero-bias result.
- Topic-term RMSE and mean-absolute parameter bias use `validation_core::summarize_recovery_metric_replications(...)`, which keeps the unconditional attempted/success/failure counts, failure rate and Bernoulli Monte Carlo standard error beside the successful-sample mean/dispersion/SE/2.5%–97.5% percentile summary. An all-failed experiment therefore retains its denominator without fabricating a scalar recovery metric. The prior `<= 1.0` probability-domain assertions remain removed because they did not constitute a practical recovery threshold.
- Truth-`K` fitted document topic states are re-expressed with the same global topic alignment, joined to simulator truth only through `ReferenceTopicFit::input().document_ids()`, and measured per rolling-origin window with simplex RMSE plus a non-cancelling mean-absolute residual. Window metrics are collapsed within each DGP replication before denominator-preserving Monte Carlo aggregation, so repeated estimates of earlier documents in expanding histories are not miscounted as independent replications.
- Truth-`K` prevalence intercept and EventTime-slope rows now use the same topic alignment before comparison. Each window's fitted coefficients are re-expressed from its frozen training `PrevalenceDesignBasis` into the simulator-owned physical EventTime basis, then measured with intercept/slope RMSE and non-cancelling mean-absolute residuals. These window metrics are likewise collapsed within each DGP replication before unconditional-denominator Monte Carlo aggregation, so changing rolling-origin centering/scaling cannot masquerade as coefficient bias.

This is the first end-to-end #680 composition contract. It does not by itself establish final empirical recovery thresholds, held-out latent-state recovery, interval/coverage calibration, production Evidence/EventTime/relation authority, accelerator parity, or release readiness.
